# Brag Plan: Freelancer Finance

## What is this app?
Freelancer Finance is a web app for independent freelancers to manage income, expenses, invoices, accounts, budgets, taxes, savings, and reports in one place.

## The angle
Make one accounting distinction feel tangible: an invoice is money owed, not money received. Follow a fictional invoice from outstanding receivable to recorded payment, then show that payment in the app's financial overview. The film treats this everyday freelance detail with calm, premium clarity.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on a clean split: **INVOICE ISSUANCE** ≠ **CASH RECEIVED**. A thin blue connector stops between the two until the payment action happens. The idea is taken directly from the README's core principle, “Invoice Issuance ≠ Cash Received.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- An invoice for a fictional sample client is marked outstanding; the frame is visibly labeled “SAMPLE DATA.”
- A cursor selects “Record Payment”; the success toast reads “Payment recorded.”
- A compact financial overview connects the payment to “Net Cash Received,” “Total Expenses,” and “Net Cash Flow,” with the app's “Income vs Expenses (6 Months)” chart.

## Outro / punchline
The dashboard resolves into the product lockup: “Freelancer Finance” and the page title's exact tagline, “Complete Financial Operations.” The quiet payoff is that a sent invoice only becomes received income after settlement.

## User flow worth showing
Open Invoices → choose “Record Payment” → see “Payment recorded.” and the resulting income reflected on the dashboard. Client and amount are fictional, clearly labeled sample data.
